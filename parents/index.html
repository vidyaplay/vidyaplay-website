Parent's Guide to the Letter Sheets

How to use our free LKG letter worksheets, in a few minutes a day.

One letter a week

Every week we add one new letter. Each letter has four sheets. You do not need to finish all four.

Start with sheets 1 and 2

  • Sheet 1: the capital letter. Trace it with a finger first, then a pencil.
  • Sheet 2: the small letter. Same steps: finger, then pencil.
  • Sheets 3 and 4 are extra. They are fun activities such as colouring, mazes, matching and puzzles. Use them when your child is bored of tracing, or as a reward at the end of the week.

The activity on sheets 3 and 4 changes from letter to letter, so every week feels new.

A simple week

  • Day 1: sheet 1, the capital letter.
  • Day 2: sheet 2, the small letter.
  • Day 3 and after: sheet 3 or 4 for fun, and say the letter sound in daily life.

About 10 minutes a day is enough. Stop when your child is tired. A short, happy session is better than a long one.

Say the name and the sound

Teach the letter name together with its sound. For example: "T, t, t as in tiger". Say it every time you trace.

Which letter first?

Follow your child's school and go A to Z, or use phonics order if you prefer. Phonics order starts with s, a, t, i, p, n, and our phonics lessons follow that order. Pick one and keep it simple.

If your child is not ready

  • Go back to tracing the letter in the air, in sand or with a finger on the sheet.
  • Do just one sheet and try again tomorrow. Never push.
  • Use a short, thick pencil or crayon.

How do I know my child has understood?

  • They can say the letter name and sound.
  • They can find the letter in a book or on a sign.
  • They can write it on their own, even if it is not neat yet.

Questions parents ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Every sheet is free, with no sign-up and no email.

Which age is it for?

The letter sheets are made for LKG, ages 3.5 to 5, and are based on the CBSE syllabus, with school-book strokes.

Can I print in black and white?

Yes. The letter sheets print well in black and white. Children can add the colour themselves.

Can I use the sheets in a class?

The sheets are for personal use at home. If you are a teacher and would like sheets for your class, write to us and we can customise them.

How often do you add new letters?

One new letter every week.

Do you collect my child's details?

No. We never ask for a name, photo or any personal details.
