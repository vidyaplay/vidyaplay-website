Blue Colour Worksheets for Nursery

Colour of the week: blue! Spot the blue things, colour a big blue sea with a whale, and draw a blue line to help the whale swim home.

Free · A4 PDF · Please print in colour · Ages 2.5 to 4

All 3 blue worksheets in one file

One page a day for a blue week.

Download the pack (PDF)
  • Worksheet 1

    Blue Things

    Circle the pictures that are blue.

    Download PDF
  • Worksheet 2

    Colour the Sea

    Colour the sea and the whale blue, the sun yellow.

    Download PDF
  • Worksheet 3

    Help the Whale Reach the Sea

    Draw a blue line along the road to the sea.

    Download PDF

Tips for parents

  • Name each picture out loud, then ask: Is it blue?
  • Go on a blue hunt at home: find 3 blue things.
  • Use fat crayons; on the road page, go slowly and stay inside.
