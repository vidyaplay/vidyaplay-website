Privacy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

VidyaPlay is a site for families and teachers. We keep privacy simple.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or login is needed to use the site or download worksheets.
  • We do not ask for any personal details, and never for children's details.
  • We do not show ads on this site at the moment.

Counting visits

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count how many people visit and which pages are popular. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.

Fonts

Our font (Baloo 2) loads from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to download it.

Links to other sites

Buttons for YouTube, Instagram and WhatsApp open those services, which have their own privacy policies.

If you email us

We only use your email address to reply to you. We do not share it.

Changes

If this page changes, for example when we add an email newsletter, we will update it here first. Questions: vidyaplay.contact@gmail.com
