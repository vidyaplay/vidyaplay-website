Colour Sort: Red and Yellow

Nursery · Colours · Ages 2.5 to 4

Red or yellow?

All sorted!

red
yellow

On a computer: press 1 for the red basket or 2 for the yellow basket, R to hear it again, M for sound on or off.

Red worksheets More games

For parents: Tap Listen, or say the picture's name and ask, "Is it red or yellow?" Your child taps the basket, or drags the picture into it. A wrong basket simply lets your child try again. Each game has 8 pictures. Nothing is collected; only your Sound on/off choice is remembered on this device. Voice made with AI.
