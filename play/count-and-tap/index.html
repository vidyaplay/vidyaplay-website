Count and Tap: 1 to 10

LKG · Counting · Ages 3 to 5

How many?

Super counting!

On a computer: press 1, 2 or 3 for the left, middle or right number, R to hear the question again, M for sound on or off.

Counting 1 to 5 worksheets Counting 6 to 10 worksheets More games

For parents: Ask "How many?" Your child touches each picture and counts out loud, then taps the number. The top row holds 5, so for bigger numbers your child can count on from 5. A wrong number simply lets your child count again; after two tries the pictures get little numbers to help. Each game has the numbers 1 to 10 once, in a mixed order. Tap Listen to hear Vidya Teacher ask the question. Nothing is collected; only your Sound on/off choice is remembered on this device. Voice made with AI.
