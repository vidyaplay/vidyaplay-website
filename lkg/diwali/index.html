Tips for parents

  • Name the pictures together first: diya, sparkler, ladoo, gift, lantern, marigold.
  • Count out loud and tick each picture as you count it.
  • Prints in black and white too: on the rangoli sheet, let your child choose their own colours.
  • Diyas and sparklers are only with a grown-up. Talk about staying safe this Diwali.
  • The pictures in this pack are made with AI art.
