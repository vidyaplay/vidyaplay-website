Brain Games Worksheets for LKG

Four looking-and-thinking puzzles for LKG: find the odd one out, match each picture to its shadow, spot the two that are the same, and work out which picture is missing.

Free · A4 PDF · Print in colour · Ages 3.5 to 5

All 4 brain games worksheets in one file

One puzzle a day for four days of thinking practice.

Tips for parents

  • Name every picture out loud together first.
  • Ask "Why?" when your child chooses an answer.
  • One puzzle a day, about 10 minutes, is plenty.
