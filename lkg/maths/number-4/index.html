Number 4 Worksheets for LKG

Four worksheets for the number 4: learn to write it with school-book strokes (down and across, then a longer line down through, open top), count and match, find it on the hen, and colour just four.

Free · A4 PDF · Prints in black and white · Ages 3.5 to 5

All 4 worksheets in one file

One sheet a day for four days of number practice.

Tips for parents

  • Say "four" and hold up four fingers every time.
  • Count four things at home: spoons, shoes, toys.
  • One sheet a day, about 10 minutes, is plenty.
