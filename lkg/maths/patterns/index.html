Patterns Worksheet: What Comes Next?

Simple AB patterns with pictures and shapes. Your child says the pattern out loud, circles what comes next, then traces the shapes to finish two patterns.

Free · A4 PDF · Prints well in black and white · Ages 3.5 to 5

  • Worksheet 1

    What Comes Next?

    Circle what comes next in 5 picture patterns, then trace shapes to finish 2 patterns.

    Download PDF

Tips for parents

  • Say the pattern out loud together: apple, banana, apple, banana...
  • Clap and tap a pattern first: clap, tap, clap, tap. What comes next?
  • Make patterns at home with spoons and cups, or red and blue blocks.
