Numbers 11 to 20 Worksheets for LKG

Six worksheets for the numbers 11 to 20: recognise each number, count carefully in rows of 10, see that 13 is 10 and 3 more, trace and write, fill the number train, and find what comes before, after and between.

Free · A4 PDF · Prints in black and white · Ages 4 to 5

All 6 worksheets in one file

One sheet a day for six days of practice.

Tips for parents

  • Say the number name together: 13 is thirteen, ten and three more.
  • Count real things in tens: 10 spoons, then add some more.
  • One sheet a day is plenty. The first one on every sheet is done for you.
