Number 2 Worksheets for LKG

Four worksheets for the number 2: learn to write it with school-book strokes, count and match, find it on the turtle, and colour just two.

Free · A4 PDF · Prints in black and white · Ages 3.5 to 5

All 4 worksheets in one file

One sheet a day for four days of number practice.

Tips for parents

  • Say "two" and hold up two fingers every time.
  • Find pairs at home: two eyes, two shoes, two hands.
  • One sheet a day, about 10 minutes, is plenty.
