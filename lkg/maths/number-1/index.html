Number 1 Worksheets for LKG

Four worksheets for the number 1: learn to write it (straight down, like the school book), count and match, find it, and colour just one.

Free · A4 PDF · Prints in black and white · Ages 3.5 to 5

All 4 worksheets in one file

One sheet a day for a week of number practice.

Tips for parents

  • Say "one" and hold up one finger every time.
  • Count real things at home: one spoon, one shoe, one apple.
  • One sheet a day, about 10 minutes, is plenty.
