Number Sequence 6 to 10: After, Before, Between and Missing

Four playful number sequence worksheets for 6 to 10: the bunny hops forward (after), the frog hops back (before), the number sandwich (between) and hopscotch (missing numbers).

Free · A4 PDF · Please print in colour · Ages 4 to 5

All 4 worksheets in one file

One sheet a day for four days.

Download the pack (PDF)
  • Worksheet 1

    Bunny Hops Forward: After

    The bunny hops one stone forward. Write the number that comes after.

    Download PDF
  • Worksheet 2

    Frog Hops Back: Before

    The frog hops one lily pad back. Write the number that comes before.

    Download PDF
  • Worksheet 3

    Number Sandwich: Between

    Write the number on the empty bread slice in the middle.

    Download PDF
  • Worksheet 4

    Hopscotch: Missing Numbers

    Hop up and hop down, and write the missing numbers.

    Download PDF

Tips for parents

  • Say the numbers in order out loud first: six, seven, eight, nine, ten.
  • Act it out: hop forward one step for after, one step back for before.
  • Do these after the 1 to 5 sheets. One sheet a day is plenty.
