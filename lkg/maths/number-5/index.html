Number 5 Worksheets for LKG

Four worksheets for the number 5: learn to write it with school-book strokes (a short line down, a round belly, then the hat on top), count and match, find it on the fish, and colour just five.

Free · A4 PDF · Prints in black and white · Ages 3.5 to 5

All 4 worksheets in one file

One sheet a day for four days of number practice.

Tips for parents

  • Say "five" and hold up five fingers every time.
  • Count five things at home: spoons, shoes, toys.
  • One sheet a day, about 10 minutes, is plenty.
