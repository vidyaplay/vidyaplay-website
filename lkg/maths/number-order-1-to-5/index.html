Number Sequence 1 to 5: After, Before, Between and Missing

Four number sequence worksheets for numbers 1 to 5: what comes after, what comes before, what comes between, and missing numbers. Each number sits in one picture, so there is nothing to count.

Free · A4 PDF · Prints well in black and white · Ages 3.5 to 5

All 4 worksheets in one file

One sheet a day for four days.

Download the pack (PDF)
  • Worksheet 1

    What Comes After 1 to 5

  • Worksheet 2

  • Worksheet 4

Tips for parents

  • Say the numbers in order out loud first: one, two, three, four, five.
  • The first one on each sheet is done for you in grey. Do it together first.
  • Do these once your child can count to 5. One sheet a day is plenty.
