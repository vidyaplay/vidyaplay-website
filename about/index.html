About VidyaPlay

Little steps. Big learning.

Made by a parent

Hi! I'm a parent of kids in LKG. When they started learning to write, I wanted worksheets that were simple, fun and easy to print at home. So I started making them myself. My kids try out the worksheets that suit their age, and I make every worksheet the way I would want it for my own children.

What you will find here

  • Free printable worksheets for Nursery, LKG and UKG, based on the CBSE syllabus.
  • A4 PDFs that are easy to print at home.
  • Fun pictures that children can colour after they finish.
  • New worksheets every week. Fun quizzes are coming soon.

Our promise

  • Every single worksheet is free. No sign-up needed to download.
  • We never ask for your child's name, photo or any personal details.
